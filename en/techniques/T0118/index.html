Extermination on a Territorial Basis

T0118 Seizure of Governance, Defense Impairment Ukrainians

Aggressors may carry out the physical extermination of a population not on the basis of the victims' political views or ethnicity, but totally — solely by virtue of their presence in a given territory. To wipe pockets of resistance off the face of the earth, carpet bombing of residential areas, deliberate mass shootings of residents, and other methods may be employed.

Procedures Carried Out3

1708 Mazepa's Defection to Sweden and the Baturyn Massacre Ukrainians

Mazepa's Defection to Sweden and the Baturyn Massacre (1708) Tsardom of Muscovy Regular Army

Total massacre of the civilian population: "Funck slaughtered more than a thousand people in the small town of Terey (Tereyska sloboda) and burned the town down, and also burned Dryhalov (Nedryhailiv)… and ordered everyone encountered to be killed, so as to instill horror in others"[1]. "In total, about 15 thousand Ukrainians died in the Baturyn tragedy, including all the women and children"[2][3].

1709 Liquidation of the Zaporozhian Sich Ukrainians

Liquidation of the Zaporozhian Sich (1709) Tsardom of Muscovy Regular Army

Total extermination of the defenders: when Perevolochna was taken, "All the Zaporozhians and inhabitants were killed (only 12 people were taken alive), and the neighboring kurins were also burned and slaughtered"[2].

2022 Full-Scale Invasion Ukrainians

Full-Scale Invasion (from February 24, 2022) Russian Federation Regular Army

Deliberate mass killings of people without regard to their ethnic origin, solely on the basis of their Ukrainian citizenship and affiliation with the Ukrainian state[4].
