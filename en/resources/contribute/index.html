We need a translator into Chechen (noxçiyn mott): we are preparing a Chechen version of the site, and it cannot be done without a native speaker. If you speak Chechen, or know someone willing to help with the translation, please write to us at contact@decolonial.ist.

Books and sources

To describe how Russia colonized the Chechens, we need scholarly publications that are hard to obtain. We are currently looking for:

  • M. M. Ibragimov (head of the author team). «История Чечни с древнейших времён до наших дней» (The History of Chechnya from Ancient Times to the Present Day): in 2 volumes. Grozny: GUP "Knizhnoe izdatelstvo", 2006–2008. ISBN 978-5-98896-103-1 (vol. 1), 978-5-98896-101-7 (vol. 2)
  • A. B. Zaks. «Ташев-Хаджи – сподвижник Шамиля» (Tashev-Hadji, an Associate of Shamil). Grozny: Kniga, 1992
  • A. G. Kavtaradze. «Генерал А. П. Ермолов» (General A. P. Yermolov). Tula: Priokskoe knizhnoe izdatelstvo, 1977 (series «Nashi slavnye zemliaki»). Google Books: 8HPNAAAAMAAJ
  • Zhizn' Natsional'nostei (the Narkomnats periodical). Moscow, 1922, No. 10(139) and No. 16 - Inal Butaev's «Грозненская нефть и Чечня» (Grozny Oil and Chechnya), No. 10, p. 6, and N. Kosterin's «Политическое состояние Горской Республики» (The Political Condition of the Mountain Republic), No. 16, p. 9. NEB record
  • «Борьба за Советскую власть в Чечено-Ингушетии (1917–1920 гг.)» (The Struggle for Soviet Power in Checheno-Ingushetia, 1917–1920: a collection of documents and materials). Grozny, 1958
  • M. D. Chentieva. «История чечено-ингушской письменности» (A History of Chechen-Ingush Writing), ed. Kh. D. Oshaev. Grozny: Checheno-Ingushskoe knizhnoe izdatelstvo, 1958. NEB ChR: 1405
  • K. I. Efanov. «Классовая борьба в чечено-ингушском ауле в период социалистического строительства» (Class Struggle in the Chechen-Ingush Aul during Socialist Construction). Grozny, 1979. NEB ChR: 222
  • A. N. Popov. «Социалистическое преобразование сельского хозяйства в Чечено-Ингушетии» (The Socialist Transformation of Agriculture in Checheno-Ingushetia). Grozny, 1976
  • «Моя Чечено-Ингушетия» (My Checheno-Ingushetia): a local-history textbook, ed. N. A. Tavakalyan. Grozny: Checheno-Ingushskoe knizhnoe izdatelstvo, 1970
  • «Репрессированные народы: чеченцы и ингуши» (Repressed Peoples: Chechens and Ingush) // «Шпион: Альманах» (the Shpion almanac). Moscow, 1993, issue 1, pp. 16–33
  • «Военно-исторический архив» (Military History Archive). Moscow, 1997, No. 2, pp. 135–136 (the North Caucasus Military District staff report on the operation in Chechnya in December 1929)
  • Published documents on the extrajudicial GPU "troikas" in Chechnya, 1930–1931
  • K. Z. Chokaev. «Тернистый путь в науку» (A Thorny Path into Science: memoirs, research materials, articles, speeches, a chronicle of events). Grozny: Groznensky rabochy, 2010. 687 pp. ISBN 978-5-900231-76-1. RSL record: 01004955272
  • «Трудовые ресурсы Чечено-Ингушетии и их рациональное использование» (Labour Resources of Checheno-Ingushetia and Their Rational Use). Grozny, 1976
  • V. V. Krylova. «К вопросу о численности и составе работников промышленности ЧИАССР в 60–80-е годы» (On the Number and Composition of Industrial Workers of the Checheno-Ingush ASSR in the 1960s–1980s) // «Актуальные проблемы…» (Current Problems…): a collection. Grozny, 1986
  • S. O. Zoev. «Развитие экономики ЧИАССР в период зрелого социализма» (The Development of the Economy of the Checheno-Ingush ASSR in the Period of Mature Socialism). Grozny, 1984
  • S. O. Zoev. «На путях перестройки» (On the Paths of Perestroika). Grozny, 1989
  • G. S. Guzhin, N. V. Chugunova. «Сельская местность Чечено-Ингушетии и её проблемы» (The Countryside of Checheno-Ingushetia and Its Problems). Grozny, 1988
  • «Грозненский рабочий» (Groznensky Rabochy): newspaper. Grozny, 1988, issues of 16 August and 12 November
  • «Живая память: о жертвах сталинских репрессий» (Living Memory: On the Victims of Stalinist Repressions), compiled by D. Khozhaev. Grozny, 1991
  • V. Yu. Zorin. «Чечня: кремнистый путь к миру: дневник не для себя» (Chechnya: The Flinty Road to Peace: A Diary Not for Myself). Moscow: Violanta, 1997. 381 pp. ISBN 5-88803-001-5. RSL record: 01001760902

If you have these books in electronic or paper form, or are willing to help us purchase them, please get in touch: contact@decolonial.ist.

Expert review

We need a professional historian – a specialist in Ukrainian history and Russian colonial policy – to review our texts on Ukrainians: fact-checking, wording, and completeness of coverage. Later on, the same kind of review will be needed for our texts on the colonization of the Chechens, which are currently under development.

Our plans include documenting the history of the colonization of other peoples that have suffered from Russian aggression, and we are looking for historians willing to join this work. If you are a historian, or can put us in touch with one, please write to us at contact@decolonial.ist.
