The First Wave of Repressions Against the "Sixtiers" (1965–1968)

C0088 august 1965 – december 1968 Ukrainians

Actor: USSR Instrument: Secret Police and Security Services

The KGB's response to the emergence of a resistance movement among the new Ukrainian intelligentsia. It includes the suppression of the 1965 protests (the public statement by Stus, Dziuba, and Chornovil at the premiere of "Shadows of Forgotten Ancestors") and the pressure on the signatories of the "Letter of 139" in 1968, in which prominent cultural figures spoke out against unlawful political trials[1]. This also includes the crushing of the underground Ukrainian National Front: "in 1967 the UNF was uncovered by the state security organs," and eight of its members received long prison terms[2].

Neutralization of the Opposition USSR Secret Police and Security Services

Targeted arrests of the new Ukrainian intelligentsia, dissidents, and protest participants (including after the premiere of "Shadows of Forgotten Ancestors") to suppress the nascent resistance[1].

T0022 Terror

Terror USSR Secret Police and Security Services

Use of political trials, interrogations, and administrative pressure by the KGB on the signatories of the "Letter of 139" to create an atmosphere of fear in cultural circles: "Administrative repressions against the 'signatories' began, along with KGB pressure. Rumors spread across Kyiv and Ukraine about the existence of an underground terrorist Banderite organization run by Western intelligence services"[3].
