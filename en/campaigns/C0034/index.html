Campaigns

Administrative Dismantling and Enserfment of the Left Bank (1781–1786)

C0034 january 1781 – december 1786 Ukrainians

Actor: Russian Empire Instruments: Government, Police Apparatus

The execution of Catherine II's secret instructions for the institutional and cultural assimilation of the land: the liquidation of the regimental order and the introduction of imperial viceroyalties – the Kyiv Viceroyalty was established by the nominal decree to the Senate of September 16, 1781 (PSZRI, coll. 1, vol. XXI, no. 15,228; execution ordered from November)[1][2], "which finally abolished the remnants of the Hetmanate's autonomy and its regimental-sotnia administrative-political and military order"[3]; the secularization of monastery lands in 1786 and the legal binding of peasants by Catherine II's decree of May 3, 1783: "each of the villagers is to remain in the place and station where he is registered by the current last revision"[4].

Procedures Carried Out3

T0030 Administrative-Territorial Division

Administrative-Territorial Division Russian Empire Government

The abolition of the Cossack regimental order and the division of the Left Bank into imperial viceroyalties: the Kyiv Viceroyalty was established by the nominal decree to the Senate of September 16, 1781 (PSZRI, coll. 1, vol. XXI, no. 15,228; execution ordered from November)[1][2], "which finally abolished the remnants of the Hetmanate's autonomy and its regimental-sotnia administrative-political and military order"[3]; the same decree established the Chernihiv and Novhorod-Siverskyi viceroyalties.

Procedure page →C0034.T0030

T0032 Legal Segregation of the Population

Legal Segregation of the Population Russian Empire Police Apparatus

The legal enserfment of Ukrainian peasants by Catherine II's decree of May 3, 1783, depriving them of the right of free movement: "For the certain and reliable collection of treasury revenues in the viceroyalties of Kyiv, Chernihiv, and Novhorod-Siverskyi and for the prevention of any flights… each of the villagers is to remain in the place and station where he is registered by the current last revision"[4].

Procedure page →C0034.T0032

T0052 Expropriation of Resources

Expropriation of Resources Russian Empire Government

The secularization and confiscation into the imperial treasury of the lands of Ukrainian Orthodox monasteries: "in 1786, during the secularization, the monastery was closed, and its property and lands passed to the state treasury"[5] – the fate of the Sviatohirsk monastery is typical of the entire 1786 campaign, which deprived the local church of its economic base.
