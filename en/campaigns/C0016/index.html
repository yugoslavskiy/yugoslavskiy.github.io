Campaigns

The Reshetylivka Articles (1709)

C0016 july 1709 – december 1709 Ukrainians

Actor: Tsardom of Muscovy Instruments: Government, Occupation and Controlled Administrations

The first systematic campaign to dismantle treaty-based agency: instead of an equal interstate treaty – the tsar's "decisive" resolutions upon the hetman's petitionary articles. "The joint document, drawn up as a decree, was signed by state chancellor Count G. Golovkin"[1] - the signature "State Chancellor, Count Golovkin. Given at Kyiv, 31 July 1709" stands under the published act itself[2]. The articles "fully preserved the previously established control of the tsarist regime over local financing, the functioning of the administrative apparatus, the Cossack army, and foreign policy"[1].

Procedures Carried Out4

T0008 Deprivation of Agency

Deprivation of Agency Tsardom of Muscovy Government

Refusal to sign a treaty: the replacement of an equal agreement with the tsar's unilateral "resolutions." "The joint document, drawn up as a decree, was signed by state chancellor Count G. Golovkin"[1][2] – the autonomy ceased to be a party to a treaty.

T0031 Restriction of Sovereignty

Restriction of Sovereignty Tsardom of Muscovy Government

Entrenchment of tsarist control over the autonomy's foreign policy: the articles "fully preserved the previously established control of the tsarist regime over… foreign policy"[1].

T0038 Economic Control

Economic Control Tsardom of Muscovy Government

Preservation of the center's control over the region's finances: the articles "fully preserved the previously established control of the tsarist regime over local financing"[1].

Procedure page →C0016.T0038

T0041 Implantation of Officials and Military Personnel

Implantation of Officials and Military Personnel Tsardom of Muscovy Occupation and Controlled Administrations

Preservation of tsarist representatives' oversight of the hetmanate's administrative apparatus: the articles "fully preserved the previously established control of the tsarist regime over local financing and the functioning of the administrative apparatus"[1]. The institution of the tsar's resident was established alongside the hetman: "The first tsar's resident was stolnik A. Izmailov, who in his work was guided by official and secret tsarist articles"; the residents "were given the right to control the election and official appointments of representatives of the starshyna"[1].
