Russo-Turkish War and the Annexation of Bessarabia (1806–1812)

C0039 january 1806 – december 1812 Ukrainians

Actor: Russian Empire Instruments: Diplomacy, Regular Army

The use of Ukrainian resources in yet another geopolitical war of the empire, which ended with the severing of Bessarabia from the Ottoman Empire: "After the incorporation of Bessarabia into Russia on May 16, 1812 (see the Treaty of Bucharest of 1812), Ukrainian Cossacks of the volunteer regiments, of the Ust-Danube Budjak Cossack Host, and of the Danube Cossack Host actively settled the Budjak"[1].

Procedures Carried Out2

T0017 Annexation of Territories

Annexation of Territories Russian Empire Diplomacy

The severing of Bessarabia from the Ottoman Empire and its official annexation under the Peace of Bucharest: the treaty "confirmed the annexation of Bessarabia to Russia and the transfer of the border from the Dniester to the Prut down to its confluence with the Danube"[2].

T0049 Forced Mobilization

Forced Mobilization Russian Empire Regular Army

The use of Ukrainian Cossack formations in the imperial war: "The Russian troops, which included the Ust-Danube Budjak Cossack Host, captured Khotyn, Bendery… Akkerman"[2].
