Establishment of the First Foothold on the Sunzha River (1567)

C1105 january 1567 – december 1567 Nokhchi (Chechens)

Actor: Tsardom of Muscovy Instrument: Regular Army

The Regular Army of the Tsardom of Muscovy built the first military base on the lands of the Nokhchi with the support of regular troops. As historian E. N. Kusheva writes, "at the request of Temriuk, conveyed in Moscow in December 1566 by his son Matlov or Mazlov, to build a town 'on the Terek River at the mouth of the Siuiunchi River' 'for protection from his enemies,' in the spring of 1567 voevodas were sent from Moscow for the town-building work — Prince A. S. Babichev and P. Protasyev 'with many men, as well as ordnance, cannons, and arquebuses'"[1]. Historians A. S. Kulikov and V. A. Runov add that the voevodas were sent "with firearms and many men"[2]. Historian E. N. Kusheva states the fact that the base was built on the lands of the Nokhchi: "The Russian town... was built that same year"[1].

T0019 Military Intervention

Military Intervention Tsardom of Muscovy Regular Army

To construct the base on the lands of the Nokhchi, the administration brought in armed detachments: historian E. N. Kusheva writes that in the spring of 1567 the voivodes A. S. Babichev and P. Protasyev were dispatched from Moscow "with many men, as well as ordnance, cannons, and arquebuses"[1], while historians A. S. Kulikov and V. A. Runov note that they were sent "with firepower and many men"[2].

Construction of Fortresses Tsardom of Muscovy Regular Army

The military forces of the Tsardom of Muscovy built a fortified outpost on the lands of the Nokhchi: historian E. N. Kusheva states that in 1567 "the Russian town... was built in that same year"[1].
