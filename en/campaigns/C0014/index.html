Liquidation of the Zaporozhian Sich (1709)

A military operation of Muscovite troops to completely destroy the Zaporozhian (Chortomlyk) Sich as an autonomous military center in revenge for its support of Charles XII: "Having lost the proxy battle for Zaporizhzhia, Peter decided to follow the path of Baturyn — to destroy the Sich"[1]. Contemporaries recorded the empire's goal directly – Orlyk wrote that Moscow intended "to eradicate the Zaporozhian Host of the Lower Dnipro and to efface its very name forever"[2].

T0077 Punitive Expeditions
January 1709
C0014 Liquidation of the Zaporozhian Sich (1709)
G0008 Tsardom of Muscovy
S0010 Regular Army

A punitive expedition against the Sich to physically annihilate the Lower Dnipro Cossacks: "Three infantry regiments led by Colonel P. Yakovlev were sent there. Keleberda was the first to be taken and burned on April 16. A stubborn battle was fought for Perevolochna"[1]. The assault on the Sich itself lasted four days. The punitive force was guided by mercenary colonel Hnat Galagan: in "1709 he took part in the destruction of the Zaporozhian Sich, for which he received four villages on the Right Bank"[3].

T0022 Terror
January 1709
C0014 Liquidation of the Zaporozhian Sich (1709)
G0008 Tsardom of Muscovy
S0010 Regular Army

Public executions of prisoners as intimidation: "Cossacks and officers were hanged, their heads were cut off, and the graves of old Zaporozhians were desecrated"[1]. The head of the acting otaman was demonstratively sent to Zaporizhzhia[1].

T0118 Extermination on a Territorial Basis
January 1709
C0014 Liquidation of the Zaporozhian Sich (1709)
G0008 Tsardom of Muscovy
S0010 Regular Army

Total extermination of the defenders: when Perevolochna was taken, "All the Zaporozhians and inhabitants were killed (only 12 people were taken alive), and the neighboring kurins were also burned and slaughtered"[1].

T0052 Expropriation of Resources
January 1709
C0014 Liquidation of the Zaporozhian Sich (1709)
G0008 Tsardom of Muscovy
S0010 Regular Army

Destruction of the military-economic base of the Lower Dnipro Cossacks. The Sich "was a kind of military base housing the fleet, artillery, and arsenal"[4] – all of this infrastructure was destroyed or seized during the assault.

T0105 Total Destruction of Infrastructure
January 1709
C0014 Liquidation of the Zaporozhian Sich (1709)
G0008 Tsardom of Muscovy
S0010 Regular Army

Complete destruction of the Sich's buildings and neighboring fortifications: "the neighboring kurins were also burned and slaughtered"; "All the buildings were destroyed, as Menshikov wrote: 'To lay waste all their places, so that this treasonous nest be utterly uprooted'"[1].

T0045 Destruction of Historical Memory
January 1709
C0014 Liquidation of the Zaporozhian Sich (1709)
G0008 Tsardom of Muscovy
S0008 Government

The issuing of an official manifesto banning the very name of the Sich in any documents[2]. Physical destruction of memory: "the graves of old Zaporozhians were desecrated"[1].

T0024 Justification Through Religion
January 1709
C0014 Liquidation of the Zaporozhian Sich (1709)
G0008 Tsardom of Muscovy
S0011 Moscow Patriarchate

The use of the church as an instrument of punishment: the anathema was proclaimed by the tsar's direct order - "be pleased to have him, for such a deed of his, publicly cursed in the cathedral church..."[2]. No theological grounding was supplied for it, and part of the Ukrainian clergy refused to accept the rite: a former Lokhvytsia archpriest said during the proclamation of the anathema, "Our Mazepa is holy and will be in heaven, and your sovereign will not"[2].
