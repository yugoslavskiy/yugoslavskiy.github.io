Campaigns

Creation of a Single "Soviet People" (1958–1970)

C0053 january 1958 – december 1970 Ukrainians

Actor: USSR Instrument: Government

The USSR's official campaign to erase national distinctions: The start was given by the USSR Law "On Strengthening the Connection of School with Life and on the Further Development of the System of Public Education in the USSR" of December 24, 1958: it contained no direct norm on language, leaving the matter to the republics[1]; the republican Law of the Ukrainian SSR of April 17, 1959, by its Article 9, made the choice of the language of instruction a matter for parents[2]. The Third Program of the CPSU, adopted by the 22nd Congress in October 1961, proclaimed "a new stage in the development of national relations in the USSR, characterized by the further drawing together of nations and the achievement of their complete unity" - up to and including "the effacement of national distinctions, especially of language distinctions"[3].

T0010 Denial of a Distinct Identity

Denial of a Distinct Identity USSR Government

The ideological course toward erasing nations: the Third Program of the CPSU, adopted by the 22nd Congress in October 1961, announced "a new stage in the development of national relations in the USSR, characterized by the further drawing together of nations and the achievement of their complete unity," and called "the effacement of national distinctions, especially of language distinctions," merely "a considerably longer process than the effacement of class boundaries"[3].

T0114.001 Imposition of Asymmetric Bilingualism and Optionality

Imposition of Asymmetric Bilingualism and Optionality USSR Government

The legislative transfer of schools through "freedom of choice": the union Law of December 24, 1958 left the language of instruction to the republics[1], and the Law of the Ukrainian SSR of April 17, 1959 wrote into Article 9: "Instruction in the schools of the Ukrainian SSR is conducted in the pupils' native language. It is for the parents to decide to which school, with which language of instruction, to send their children" - the study of a language not taught in a given school became optional[2].

T0158 Territorial Marking with Imperial Symbols

Territorial Marking with Imperial Symbols USSR Government

The material sign of the "merging of nations" was put up in Pereiaslav in the Kyiv region, the city of the Pereiaslav Rada: "on 8 October 1961, a majestic monument in honor of the 300th anniversary of the reunification of Ukraine with Russia was unveiled on B. Khmelnytsky Square"[4]. The authorities unveiled the sign themselves: according to the newsreel Radianska Ukraina No. 49, the opening rally was addressed by Petro Shelest, first secretary of the Kyiv obkom of the CPU, and Pankratov, a secretary of the Moscow obkom of the party - the metropole spoke at the unveiling in its own voice[5]. The composition consisted of two female figures representing Ukraine and Russia and three bas-reliefs with medallions: "a torch, 1654; a hammer and sickle in the rays of the sun, 1917; the Order of Victory, 1945"[6]. The sign's purpose is recorded in its own protection passport: "The monument is a symbol of the eternal, unbreakable friendship and the indestructible monolithic unity of the fraternal peoples - Ukrainian and Russian…"[7]. Four years after the unveiling, the sign was taken under state protection by the resolution of the Council of Ministers of the Ukrainian SSR of 21 July 1965 No. 711 "On the approval of the list of monuments of art, history and archaeology of the Ukrainian SSR"[8][9]. The pedestal bore the inscription "Forever together - forever with the Russian people"; in early December 2021 it was removed by unknown persons[10], and the monument itself was dismantled on 7 July 2022 - by an order of the Ministry of Culture that struck the monument, protection No. 5894-Ko, off the register "due to the loss of the subject of protection"[11].
