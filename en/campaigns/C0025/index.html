The Konotop Articles of Ivan Samoilovych (1672)

C0025 june 1672 – december 1672 Ukrainians

Actor: Tsardom of Muscovy Instrument: Diplomacy

A further curtailment of the Hetmanate's political agency and interference in the autonomy's internal affairs: "Overall, the Konotop Articles limited the hetman's power in favor of the officer oligarchy, and also narrowed the autonomy of the Hetmanate"[1]. The weakening of the hetman's power laid the ground for the growing influence of the Muscovite government over the autonomy's elites.

Procedures Carried Out2

T0015 Bribery of Elites

Bribery of Elites Tsardom of Muscovy Diplomacy

A wager on the estate interests of the officer class: "The Konotop Articles limited both the hetman's power and, accordingly, the autonomy of the Hetmanate, while significantly expanding the rights of the Cossack officers"[2] – by expanding the rights of the officer oligarchy at the hetman's expense, Moscow gained levers of influence over the autonomy through the hands of its own elite.

Procedure page →C0025.T0015

T0031 Restriction of Sovereignty

Restriction of Sovereignty Tsardom of Muscovy Diplomacy

Interference in internal governance: "the hetman was deprived of the right to arbitrarily (without a hearing before the military court) issue orders for the execution or dismissal of his subordinates"[2] – personnel and judicial decisions were removed from the hetman's sole authority. The norm itself is older than this treaty: it was first fixed by the Konotop Articles of 1672 ("executed and removed from office… by the judgment and proof of the Host's court")[3].
