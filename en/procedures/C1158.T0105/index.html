Total Destruction of Infrastructure

C1158.T0105 december 1836 Nokhchi (Chechens)

Actor: Russian Empire Instrument: Regular Army

On 29-31 December 1836, Pullo's detachment completely destroyed Kishkeroy-Yurt: "the village of Kishkeroy, consisting of 63 households, with a considerable stock of grain and hay, was put to the torch", and on withdrawing on 31 December Pullo "marched out of Kishkeroy-Yurt, having set fire to the houses that remained undestroyed"[1].
