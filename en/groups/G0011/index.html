Russian Federation

A state formation since 1991. It acts as an actor employing both inherited imperial instruments of hard power (military intervention, puppet governments, mass killings) and modern methods of control (economic and transport blockades, proxy wars through security services, cyberattacks, networked propaganda, and indoctrination).

Procedures Carried Out


September 2004
T0021 Neutralization of the Opposition
C0096 Election Interference and Countering the "Orange Revolution" (2004)
G0011 Russian Federation
S0017 Secret Police and Security Services

An attempt to physically eliminate the leader of the democratic opposition V. Yushchenko at the height of the election campaign by dioxin poisoning: in late December 2004 "a patient presented with TCDD poisoning; the levels in his blood serum (108000 pg/g lipid weight) were more than 50 000-fold greater than those in the general population"[1]. The poisoning led to severe disfigurement of the candidate's face.

September 2004
T0006 Dehumanization
C0096 Election Interference and Countering the "Orange Revolution" (2004)
G0011 Russian Federation
S0021 Propaganda

The conduct of an aggressive information campaign in favor of "their" candidate with the direct participation of the head of the Russian state: live on three Ukrainian television channels two weeks before the vote, praise was voiced for the candidate's government - "V. Yanukovych's government achieved more: they managed to fill this growth with good quality. That is, Ukraine's economy is developing in a diversified way"[2]. In parallel, opponents were demonized with accusations of nationalism and pro-fascist views.

September 2004
T0012 Support for Separatism
C0096 Election Interference and Countering the "Orange Revolution" (2004)
G0011 Russian Federation
S0009 Diplomacy

The instigation of the threat of splitting the country in response to the protests: at the congress in Sievierodonetsk on November 28, 2004, Regions faction leader R. Bohatyriova gave a direct promise of a separatist referendum - "we will not go to it with you, we will hold a referendum on the creation of a South-Eastern state (ovation, applause)"[3]. The presence of Russian officials lent this Moscow's sanction: the mayor of Moscow Yu. Luzhkov reminded the hall from the rostrum that "our President of Russia Vladimir Vladimirovich Putin was the first to congratulate Viktor Fedorovych Yanukovych on his victory"[3].

January 2006
T0108 Economic Blockade
C0097 Gas Wars and Energy Blackmail (2006–2009)
G0011 Russian Federation
S0009 Diplomacy

Direct energy blackmail and the cut-off of gas supplies at the height of winter: on January 1, 2006 - "at 10.00am Moscow time on January 1, 2006, Gazprom cut off gas supplies to Ukraine"[4]; on January 1, 2009 - "Russian exports to Ukraine were cut off on 1 January," after which exports to the EU were "drastically reduced on 6 January and cut completely from 7 January," and in the worst-affected Balkan countries a humanitarian emergency ensued, with "parts of the populations unable to heat their homes"[5].

January 2006
T0015 Bribery of Elites
C0097 Gas Wars and Energy Blackmail (2006–2009)
G0011 Russian Federation
S0017 Secret Police and Security Services

The imposition of corrupt gas-supply schemes through intermediary companies: Moscow "used a series of intermediary trading companies (Itera from 1998, Eural Trans Gas from 2003, Rosukrenergo (RUE) from 2005) to transport, and sometimes to supply, the gas to Ukraine," a practice that "was long criticized in European government circles and by corporate governance bodies (where there was concern about their opacity)"[5]. RUE's co-owner Dmytro Firtash told the US ambassador in December 2008 outright that "He acknowledged ties to Russian organized crime figure Seymon Mogilevich, stating he needed Mogilevich's approval to get into business in the first place"[6].

April 2008
T0039 Collusion with a Third Party Behind the People's Back
C0098 Diplomatic Attack Against NATO Enlargement (2008)
G0011 Russian Federation
S0009 Diplomacy

An attempt to strike a deal with the US leader over Kyiv's head in order to block Ukraine's Euro-Atlantic integration under the threat of its dismemberment: Ukraine's accession to NATO would create "a field of conflict for you and us, long-term confrontation," while Moscow, relying on anti-NATO forces, "would be working on stripping NATO of the possibility of enlarging. Russia would be creating problems there all the time"[7]. As retold by Kommersant's source from the summit itself: "You understand, George, that Ukraine is not even a state! What is Ukraine? Part of its territory is Eastern Europe, and part - a considerable part - was gifted by us!" - with a transparent hint "that Russia could begin the severance of Crimea and Eastern Ukraine"[8].

April 2008
T0137 Disinformation
C0098 Diplomatic Attack Against NATO Enlargement (2008)
G0011 Russian Federation
S0021 Propaganda

The deliberate injection of false historical narratives into the international arena: Ukraine, according to the Russian leader, "is not a nation built in a natural manner" and "an artificial country created back in Soviet times," and its territories were supposedly gifts - "In the 1920s and 1930s Ukraine obtained territory from Russia -- that's the eastern part of the country. In 1956, the Crimean peninsula was transferred to Ukraine"[7]. From this the thesis of its possible disintegration was derived: "the country could just split apart"[7].

January 2012
T0113.002 Exclusion of the Native Language from Official Use
C0055 The Kivalov-Kolesnichenko Law (2012)
G0011 Russian Federation
S0020 Controlled Opposition

An attempt to legalize the marginalization of the state language in the regions: the law "On the Principles of State Language Policy" (the Kivalov-Kolesnichenko law) allowed record-keeping to be transferred to "regional languages," effectively entrenching the dominance of Russian in the Russified regions[9].

October 2013
T0015 Bribery of Elites
C0099 Derailing the Association with the EU (2013–2014)
G0011 Russian Federation
S0009 Diplomacy

The use of economic blackmail and promises of a loan together with a gas discount to coerce V. Yanukovych into derailing the signing of the Association Agreement with the EU: on December 17, 2013, a reduction of the gas price "to 268.5 dollars per thousand cubic meters" (instead of 410) was announced, along with the fact that "Ukraine, under the bilateral agreements, will receive 15 billion dollars from the Russian National Welfare Fund, selling Moscow its government securities for that amount." Putin stressed: "We consider this a temporary solution, bearing in mind that long-term, long-lasting agreements must and will be reached"[10].

February 2014
T0019 Military Intervention
C0100 Beginning of the Russo-Ukrainian War: Armed Invasion and Occupation of Crimea (2014)
G0011 Russian Federation
S0010 Regular Army

The use of regular troops without insignia to establish control over the peninsula. A year later V. Putin admitted it on his call-in show of 17 April 2014: "behind the backs of Crimea's self-defense forces, our servicemen, of course, stood. They acted very correctly, but… decisively and professionally"[11]. Ukrainian garrisons on the peninsula were blockaded and disarmed, as Putin himself admitted: "and the way the Ukrainian units were disarmed - all of this was done so swiftly that it seemed it had been prepared for an incredibly long time"[11].

February 2014
T0137 Disinformation
C0100 Beginning of the Russo-Ukrainian War: Armed Invasion and Occupation of Crimea (2014)
G0011 Russian Federation
S0021 Propaganda

The official denial of Russian troops' participation during the operation – and Putin's later admission (call-in show, 17 April 2014) that the denial was a lie: "behind the backs of Crimea's self-defense forces, our servicemen, of course, stood"[11]. In the same broadcast an interlocutor congratulated him on having "honestly said that the 'little green men' are our peaceful military"[11].

February 2014
T0037 Puppet Government
C0100 Beginning of the Russo-Ukrainian War: Armed Invasion and Occupation of Crimea (2014)
G0011 Russian Federation
S0017 Secret Police and Security Services

The installation of a controlled administration under military cover: the decisions of the Crimean parliament were taken in conditions where "behind the backs of Crimea's self-defense forces, our servicemen, of course, stood"[11]. The UN General Assembly declared the ensuing referendum void and confirmed that it "was not authorized by Ukraine"[12]. OHCHR recorded the "election" scene itself: overnight into 27 February "armed groups without insignia took over the buildings of the local government and parliament," and "on 27 February, members of the Parliament of Crimea, in the presence of gunmen, dismissed the local Government and elected Sergey Aksenov as the Head of Crimea"[13].

The manufacturing of an appearance of mass pro-Russian mobilization on the peninsula: behind the "self-defense forces," in whose name the operation was conducted, stood Russian troops, as Putin admitted on 17 April 2014: "behind the backs of Crimea's self-defense forces, our servicemen, of course, stood"[12]. Rally and "volunteer" crowds supplied the picture of a popular impulse whose legal significance the UN rejected[11].

April 2014
T0018 Proxy War
C0101 Continuation of the Russo-Ukrainian War: Armed Aggression in the Donbas (2014–2015)
G0011 Russian Federation
S0017 Secret Police and Security Services

The seizure of Sloviansk by an armed group that had crossed the border, and the formation of the separatist "DNR"/"LNR": the detachment's commander openly admitted that "in practice it was our detachment that set in motion the flywheel of the war that is still going on," and that "it was I, after all, who pulled the trigger of the war"[15]. In parallel, "groups of armed men unlawfully carried out a well-organized and coordinated seizure of public buildings and police and security service offices"[16]. The "republic's" leadership was from the outset dispatched from Moscow: "Alexander Boroday - the first prime minister of the DNR, who in 2014 handed over his powers to Alexander Zakharchenko," a Muscovite raised "in an intelligentsia Moscow family"[17].

April 2014
T0137 Disinformation
C0101 Continuation of the Russo-Ukrainian War: Armed Aggression in the Donbas (2014–2015)
G0011 Russian Federation
S0021 Propaganda

A massive campaign of fabricating fakes to dehumanize Ukrainians: on state television it was claimed that "Ukrainian armed forces herded city residents to the central square and publicly executed a small boy and his mother," yet "there is no evidence to support Pyshniak's claim," and the residents of Sloviansk who were interviewed did not confirm the story[18]. The same method produced the fabricated "death of a 10-year-old girl" in the Petrovskyi district of Donetsk (March 2015): BBC correspondents on the ground established that no such girl had ever existed[19]. In parallel ran an operation to conceal responsibility for the destruction of flight MH17: the Russian Ministry of Defense "made a series of self-contradictory claims blaming Ukraine, rather than Russia or Russian-led separatists, for the Boeing 777's shoot down," while a deputy defense minister publicly repeated "a fabricated claim that originated on Twitter of how a Spanish air traffic controller named Carlos saw two Ukrainian fighter jets near MH17"[20].

April 2014
T0136 Cyber Operations
C0101 Continuation of the Russo-Ukrainian War: Armed Aggression in the Donbas (2014–2015)
G0011 Russian Federation
S0017 Secret Police and Security Services

The use of a hacker group linked to the security services to compromise Ukrainian artillerymen's software: "the filename 'Попр-Д30.apk' was linked to a legitimate application which was initially developed domestically within Ukraine by an officer of the 55th Artillery Brigade named Yaroslav Sherstuk," and "in-depth reverse engineering revealed the APK contained an Android variant of X-Agent" - an implant whose exclusive operator is FANCY BEAR[21]. The same toolkit was used in cyberattacks on the Ukrainian power grid in the winters of 2015 and 2016[22][23].

April 2014
T0019 Military Intervention
C0101 Continuation of the Russo-Ukrainian War: Armed Aggression in the Donbas (2014–2015)
G0011 Russian Federation
S0010 Regular Army

The overt crossing of the state border by regular Russian troops in the summer and autumn of 2014 to save the separatists from defeat: the "DNR" commander admitted that the decisive role was played by Russian servicemen who came "on leave" - "it was mostly 'vacationers' who advanced on Mariupol. When they left, both the front line and the opportunities became precarious"[15]. The offensive resulted in heavy losses for the Ukrainian army near Ilovaisk and Debaltseve. The presence of regular troops continued into 2015: the GRU men captured near Shchastia were "soldiers of the 3rd Separate Guards Special Purpose Brigade of the Main Directorate of the General Staff of the Russian Armed Forces, who arrived on the territory of Ukraine on March 26 and were quartered in Luhansk"[24].

T0020 Staging a Coup

G0011 Russian Federation

January 2020
T0004 Rewriting of History
C0102 Ideological Preparation for the Full-Scale Invasion (2020–2021)
G0011 Russian Federation
S0009 Diplomacy

The publication of the manifesto "On the Historical Unity of Russians and Ukrainians" (July 2021), constructing a pseudo-historical myth: "Russians and Ukrainians are one people, a single whole," while modern borders are declared the result of the fact that "the Bolsheviks' actions in severing from Russia its historical territories are not considered a criminal act"[28]. The same article describes how Lenin personally "persuaded" the leaders of the Donetsk-Kryvyi Rih republic "to act within Soviet Ukraine," and how the Central Committee resolved "to create at the congress 'one government for all of Ukraine'"[28]. On the eve of the invasion the thesis was taken to its limit: in his address of February 21, 2022 Putin declared that "modern Ukraine was entirely and wholly created by Russia, or, to be more precise, by Bolshevik, communist Russia," and called the country "Ukraine named after Vladimir Ilyich Lenin" - "He is its author and architect"[29].

February 2022
T0158 Territorial Marking with Imperial Symbols
C0103 Full-Scale Invasion (from February 24, 2022)
G0011 Russian Federation
S0012 Occupation and Controlled Administrations

From April 2022 the Russian occupation administrations marked the seized territories with imperial signs - in stone and on paper. In Kherson oblast more than 110 Lenin monuments had been dismantled before the invasion, with practically none left; after the occupation the invaders erected ten new ones: the first in Henichesk in April 2022 - the first Lenin monument on Ukrainian territory since 2014 - and at the end of that same month a monument was returned to its pedestal by the city council in Nova Kakhovka[36]. In Mariupol on 5 May 2022 a monument to a woman with a red flag was unveiled, and the ceremony was published in his Telegram channel by Andrei Turchak, secretary of the general council of "United Russia"; the Square of the Warrior-Liberators, named in honor of those who freed the city from the Nazis, was given back its Soviet name of "Lenin Komsomol Square" by the occupiers[37]. In parallel the occupation administration papered the territory with banners: in Kherson and Nova Kakhovka hung Russian-language billboards reading "Russia is here forever!," "Kherson - forever with Russia," "Kherson is a Russian city," and for them they bought, for twenty cents on a stock-photo site, a picture by the Ukrainian photographer Anna Pasichnyk - her son Yaroslav with a friend in vyshyvankas in a rapeseed field near Kyiv - painting a Russian flag in behind the children's backs[38]. In Balakliia in Kharkiv oblast the occupiers pasted over a city banner: first they hung "Ukraine and Russia are 'one'" on top of it, then a poster with a tricolor reading "We are one people with Russia!". On 10 September 2022 Ukrainian soldiers tore the tricolor down - and beneath it emerged the lines of Shevchenko that had stood there through the entire occupation: "Fight - and you shall prevail! God is helping you! Truth is with you, glory is with you, and holy freedom!"[39]. The signs held only until de-occupation: on 11 November 2022, a "Russia is here forever" poster in liberated Bilozerka in the Kherson region was torn down by local residents - the video was published by the Telegram channel "Nikolaevsky Vanek"[40].
